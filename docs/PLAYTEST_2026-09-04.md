## 5. Machine notes

- **Godot steals focus on every `run_project`.** It cost a lot of interruptions
  this session. Worth solving before the next one.
- The godot-mcp client does not reliably reconnect if a previous debug instance
  is still alive. What works: kill every instance whose window title contains
  `DEBUG`, confirm port 9090 is free, then one `run_project`, then wait ~15 s.
- **Never `game_eval` a method without checking its arity.** `_on_deployed_activated()`
  takes a `GhostTarget`; calling it bare broke into the debugger and wedged the
  game permanently, not for 30 s.
- **Screenshots lag the eval by seconds.** Any beat shorter than about 5 s has to
  be caught with `get_tree().paused = true` immediately before the screenshot.
- The kit-check brief reopens a frame or two after boot, so
  `_force_close_blocking_screens()` has to be called twice with a wait between.
