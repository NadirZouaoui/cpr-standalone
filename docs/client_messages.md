## Upwork / email update

**Milestone title:** LVR & CPR Simulation — revised build

Hi [Name],

New build, with your notes worked through. Same run, about 10 minutes.

**Attached:** `LVR CPR.exe` — double-click, nothing to install.

Taking your points in order:

- **Naming the kit.** You name each item as you go, what you have named is listed on
  screen, and you can correct a wrong one there and then. The correction is noted in the
  debrief - both what was said first and what it was settled at.
- **The hazards.** Now a tick-through list rather than hunting round the room, and it is
  asked twice: once from the floor, and again once the board is open and the busbars are
  exposed. The order of the lines is scrambled each run so nobody can learn the shape of
  the answer.
- **Gloves and torch before the work starts.** Both are assessed as part of preparing, so
  there is light the moment the power goes.
- **Fire.** You have to establish he is not alight before you touch him, and the fire
  blanket in the kit is the answer if he is.
- **The shirt stays shut.** That step is gone.
- **The airway.** Look, listen and feel at the ear, then roll him to check the airway for
  a blockage. Clear, and you check for a pulse. No pulse, straight into compressions.
- **Once there is a pulse.** Back onto his side in the recovery position, and you check him
  over for other injuries while help is on the way. The ambulance arrives with the siren
  and takes over.

Two things from my side that you will see straight away: a new opening card showing the
three parts of the exercise with a picture for each, and it now loads under the ROAR logo
like the substation one. The on-screen prompts have also been tidied so they stop
repeating themselves and stop overlapping each other.

**One for you:** the two run-ending mistakes are still the two we agreed. If your standard
treats any others as fatal, they are a small change.

Thanks,
Nadir

## Upwork milestone submission

**Milestone title:** LVR & CPR Simulation — playable build

Hi [Name],

The LVR & CPR exercise is ready for you to test. It's a complete run, start to finish — about
10 minutes.

**Attached:**

1. `LVR CPR.exe` — just double-click it, nothing to install.
2. `LVR_CPR_Walkthrough.pdf` — three pages: the controls, the correct sequence step by step,
   and the mistakes the exercise is designed to catch.

The trainee is the safety observer on a live job. They pick out the rescue kit, isolate the
hazard, witness the shock, break contact with the insulated crook, drag the casualty clear,
then run the primary survey and do CPR with an AED. Everything they do is graded and comes
back in a debrief at the end.

It's a big step up from the last exercise:

- **No more menus.** Everything is done hands-on in the room — the radio on the bench, the
  crook on the rack, the pads on the chest. Prompts appear on the part of the body they
  relate to.
- **The compressions are properly measured**, for both depth and rhythm, against a metronome.
  Thirty shallow ones no longer score the same as thirty good ones.
- **It assesses decisions, not just actions.** Wrong pad placement is accepted in silence, and
  there's a plausible wrong move offered after the shock. Nothing is corrected in the moment —
  it all comes out in the debrief, which now shows their sequence beside the correct one.
- **The AED talks you through it**, the way a real unit does.

Two things that would help most from your side: does the sequence match your training
standard, and are the two run-ending mistakes the right two? Both are easy to change now.

Happy to walk you through it on a call if that's easier.

Thanks,
Nadir
