## 1. What changed since `CPR_HANDOFF.md` was written

Its §5 open issues 1, 2, 4 and 6 are **done**. Issues 3 (editor gizmos) and 5 (yellow
breaker) are still open and still not blockers.

### Camera and player
- `cpr_camera_rig.gd` captures `_camera_rest_xform` in `_take_camera()` and restores it in
  `release()`. This was the "player is low to the ground and the camera tilts" bug — the
  rig was handing back a corrupted transform.
- Look clamps widened: `look_yaw_limit_deg = 110`, pitch −80…+55, so the AED is reachable
  from the kneel anchor.
- `release_smooth(duration)` + `signal released()` — used by the stand-up control so the
  handback tweens instead of snapping.
- `player.gd`: `camera_fov = 60.0` (vertical, under `KEEP_HEIGHT`; Godot's 75 default is
  ~107° horizontal at 16:9 and made the casualty look small and far away). `safe_margin =
  0.02` and a `_headroom_shape` test so the capsule only grows back when there is room —
  fixes wedging under the cabinet.

### CPR phase
- Centre prompts exist for every state (`CprStation.STATE_MESSAGES`) and double as action
  receipts. `AED_FETCH` is no longer silent.
- Stand-up control: `[C]` (the `crouch` action) leaves the anchor during `AED_FETCH` and
  `SHOCK`; pressing it again during `SHOCK` returns to the anchor. The bottom-left chip is
  `cpr_key_hint.gd`, reading the real binding via `CprStation.key_name_for()`.
- 3D `FpsArms` were **cut**. Replaced by `cpr_hands_2d.gd` — drawn translucent hands,
  world-anchored to `CprRig` (so they stay on the sternum while the look stays free),
  sized from a real-world hand width, driven by `CasualtyCpr.current_depth()`.
- Compressions require aiming at the casualty; the hands double as the aim indicator.
- Casualty hull moved to collision layer 4: the body is walk-through but still ray-hit.
- Palette consolidated from cyan to the game's gold (`Tokens.ATTENTION`) across ghosts,
  prompts and the shock button.
- Fast-forward past rep 10 is animated, not skipped, and is
  `var fast_forward_enabled := OS.is_debug_build()` — **remove at ship**, the trainee does
  all 30.
- STAND CLEAR / PRESS SHOCK no longer flash (the alternating branch in `cpr_panel_3d.gd`
  is gone).
- The floor AED's separate pad meshes ghost along with the drop preview.

### The chest shading artefact (fixed — three real causes, not one)
Reported as "a stray polygon pointing up that moved with the chest", normal in Blender.
Fixed in `casualty_cpr.gd`:
1. Blend shapes are a whole-mesh property, so the normal-mapped clothing surfaces re-lit
   on every compression → `repair_mesh()` moves surfaces no blend shape touches
   onto a child `CasualtyStaticSurfaces`.
2. Four duplicate coincident triangles → `_strip_duplicate_faces()`.
3. Seam normals wrong → `_recompute_normals()`, keyed on quantised position.

Those three repairs also clear the dark spot on the teeth — but they only ran when the
compressions phase built `CasualtyCpr`, so every earlier beat showed the broken mesh.
`repair_mesh()` is now static and `main.gd::_repair_casualty_mesh()` calls it at startup;
`_build()` calling it again is a no-op.

### The black patches under the arms (fixed)
Not geometry. Every clothing material imports with `metallic = 1.0` and its
`metallic_texture` pointing at the *roughness* map — glTF expects metal in that texture's
blue channel, and these are plain greyscale roughness maps, so the shirt renders as a
metal of ~0.79. Metal with nothing to reflect (Compatibility: no GI, no probes) goes black
in any crevice, and the armpits are the deepest crevice on a body with its arms out. The
character pack's real metalness maps are black (means 0.007–0.02), so the fix is
`metallic = 0.0` in `scripts/casualty/mesh_repair.gd::fix_clothing_metalness()`, called
from main.gd for both bodies. Ruled out first, do not repeat: recomputing normals
(wholesale and outlier-only), forcing `CULL_BACK`, and reversing winding — the region is
FRONT_FACING and consistently wound, it was never a normals problem.

The same defect is on the room's `Iron`, `Fabric Rough 01` and `Material.0xx` materials.
Deliberately left alone — only the two casualty meshes are touched.

Both bodies are repaired: `Casualty_CPR_Posed` and the clothed worker `Boots1_002`.
`tools/check_casualty_geometry.tscn` is the regression check for all of it.

Dead ends already tried, do not repeat: `BLEND_SHAPE_MODE_RELATIVE` (makes the whole body
translate — the importer stores **absolute** vertex positions, so NORMALIZED is correct),
epsilon-pinning the shape weight, and repairing only the outlier normals.

### DRSABCD restructure
`resources/procedures/lvr_cpr_procedure.tres` was rewired to DRSABCD order and 10 orphaned
steps retired; 17 remain. The `.tres` freeze in `CPR_CONTRACT.md` §7 was lifted by the
user for this.

`casualty_action_menu.gd` was rewritten as a **diegetic world-space card**: a SubViewport
on a fully-billboarded quad parented into the scene (the node in `main.tscn` stays a
`CanvasLayer`, because that file is frozen and `CprStation` resolves it by name). Rows are
picked by ray-plane intersection against the camera's own basis — no collider, no physics
query. It deliberately does **not** call `Events.open_ui()`; the mouse stays captured. It
closes after a 2.5 s linger, and a second pick inside that window keeps it open.

Rows now: Check for a response · Open the airway — full head tilt · Expose the chest ·
Stand up.

### Calling for help is now a physical act (most recent work)
- New `scripts/interaction/emergency_radio.gd`. The two-way radio on the bench (Blender
  object `Radio1`) completes **both** `send_for_help` and `call_for_aed` in one use —
  dispatching someone for an AED is part of the same radio call, and two presses of the
  same object would be busywork.
- Bound by `tool_rack.gd::_bind_radio()`. It was removed from the `TOOLS` pickup table, so
  it is no longer carryable. `id`/`display_name` are set **before** `add_child`, because
  `Interactable._ready()` warns on a missing id and `_ready_impl()` runs after that check.
- Bound via `preload()`, not the `class_name` — a newly added script is not in the global
  class cache until the editor rescans, and this must work headless.
- Silent during the kit check (`not SimState.is_preamble()`), the same rule `PickupItem`
  follows. The same mesh is still a kit-naming question in the preamble; `InteractionRay`
  prefers whichever `Interactable` can currently interact.
- `prompt_text()` returns `""` once used. `InteractionRay._resolve()` falls back to an
  *unavailable* Interactable rather than to nothing, and `_set_current()` early-returns
  when `next == current` — so a used one-shot keeps re-emitting its prompt until the
  crosshair moves off it. Every other one-shot hides its mesh on use, so this had never
  shown. Worth knowing before adding another persistent one-shot.
- Prompting: on `step_completed(check_response)` it emits "Call for help — use the two-way
  radio on the bench", after a 3.6 s delay so it lands behind the "No response" message,
  and repeats every 12 s until the call is placed.
- The call row was removed from the casualty menu and the card height trimmed to 372 px.

## 3. How to test — this is the fast loop, use it

Headless regression; exits 0 and prints `all assertions passed`:

```bash
"C:/Program Files/Godot.exe" --headless --path . res://scripts/cpr/cpr_headless_test.tscn
```

Live inspection: godot-mcp `run_project`, wait ~8 s for the interaction server, then
`game_eval`. Screenshots from inside the game:

```gdscript
get_viewport().get_texture().get_image().save_png("<scratchpad>/shot.png")
```

**Staging traps that cost time this session:**
- The kit-check modal blocks the ray at startup. Clear it with
  `get_tree().current_scene.get_node("DevMenu")._force_close_blocking_screens()`.
- Teleporting the player: set `global_position` with feet near y ≈ 0.05 and zero the
  velocity, or it falls out of the world. Placing it "1 m from X" can land it outside the
  building — offset toward `CprAnchor` to stay inside.
- Aiming: the player yaws the **body** and pitches the **head**. `head.look_at()` writes a
  full basis and fights that. Zero `head.rotation.y`/`.z`, then
  `p.rotation.y = atan2(-d.x, -d.z)` and `p.head.rotation.x = asin(d.y)`.
- Never `game_eval` a standalone lambda or a method the object may not have
  (e.g. `QuadMesh.get_blend_shape_count()`) — it breaks into the debugger and hangs the
  game for 30 s.
- `[F10]` dev menu can warp straight to chest-exposed; `[F8]` is the render-triangle probe;
  `[F9]` is the collider probe.

## 4. Rules that keep biting

All from `CPR_HANDOFF.md` §6, repeated because each has cost a session:

- **Bind room nodes by name at runtime**, from `call_deferred("_build")`. The room is an
  imported `.blend` and its subtree is rebuilt wholesale on reimport, so editor NodePaths
  go stale silently. `CprGhost.find_node` / `find_nodes_with_prefix` exist for this.
- `owner = null` on every code-added node so it is never serialised.
- `Time.get_ticks_msec()` for gameplay timing, never `delta`.
- Mouse stays `MOUSE_MODE_CAPTURED`. No `Area3D` mouse picking. Everything goes through
  the crosshair and the `interact` action.
- Collision layers: 1 World, 2 Interactable, 3 Casualty, 4 Detail. Player layer 1 / mask 1;
  `InteractionRay` mask 7.
- Godot winding: `cross(v1-v0, v2-v0)` points **away** from the shading normal. Negate it.
- `main.tscn` and `events.gd` remain frozen. `lvr_cpr_procedure.tres` is not.
