## Goal
Add the agreed gotchas to the LVR CPR sim: misdirecting/withheld prompts where grading already exists, one new diegetic choice card, and one new post-shock pill. No new signals (events.gd frozen), main.tscn untouched (cards spawned from main.gd).

## 1. Prompt-suppression mechanism
- `scripts/core/procedure_step.gd`: add three exported fields — `display_as: StringName` (&"" = show own title), `suppress_prompt: bool`, `urgent: bool`.
- `scripts/ui/hud.gd`: `_refresh_step_pill()` and `_refresh_checklist()` resolve a helper that follows `display_as` and hides suppressed steps. The checklist "current" row obeys the same resolution so the list never leaks a hidden step.

## 2. Gotcha — PPE and crook unprompted; urgent flashing break-contact prompt
- In `lvr_cpr_procedure.tres`: retitle `contact_broken` to **"Break contact with the live hazard"** (no crook mention — this title then reads consistently in the pill, checklist and debrief). Set `urgent = true` on it, and `display_as = &"contact_broken"` on both `ppe_donned` and `crook_retrieved`.
- Effect: the moment the worker is revealed in contact (phase BREAK_CONTACT, contact clock running), the pill reads "Break contact with the live hazard" — PPE and hook are never mentioned.
- Urgent styling in `hud.gd`: while the resolved current step is `urgent`, the step pill switches to the danger stylebox (`Tokens.DANGER` red, white text) and flashes via a looping modulate tween (killed/restarted on every refresh, respects `_sync_visibility`). The checklist's current row takes the DANGER colour (steady, no flash). All other steps keep the normal glass pill.
- Grading untouched: PPE missed = critical fail; crook taken before gloves = out-of-order warning; bare hands on the casualty = existing fatal.

## 3. Gotcha — isolation unprompted (no card, order unchanged)
- Procedure keeps drag-then-isolate. `supply_isolated.suppress_prompt = true`.
- After the drag the pill goes quiet; the trainee isolates at their own discretion. Whenever they throw the breaker it is graded exactly as today: before the drag = out-of-order warning (existing breaker_handle early-throw path), after = on time. `check_response` still unlocks only once the supply is isolated.

## 4. Gotcha — no prompt after airway open
- `chest_exposed.suppress_prompt = true`, `breathing_checked.suppress_prompt = true`. Their body pills ("Open the shirt" / "Check for breathing") are the only prompts; the trainee decides the order.

## 5. Radio nag removed + help choice card
- `scripts/interaction/emergency_radio.gd`: delete the 3.6 s delayed nag and the 12 s repeating reminder (timer + `_remind()`). Radio keeps its crosshair prompt and still completes `send_for_help` + `call_for_aed` on use, whenever that happens.
- New generic component `scripts/ui/choice_card_3d.gd`, modelled on `kit_identify_panel.gd` (SubViewport on a billboard quad, glass pills, gold hover, ray-plane crosshair picking, shrinks to max screen fraction). API: `open(anchor: Node3D, heading: String, choices: PackedStringArray)`, `close()`, signal `choice_made(index, label)`.
- Help card instance wired in `main.gd`: opens once on `step_completed(check_response)` (skipped if the radio was already used), anchored above the casualty — heading "The casualty is not breathing. What do you do first?" with choices **"Call for help"** and **"Perform CPR"**.
  - Call for help (correct): completes `prioritised_help`, then one single centre receipt "The two-way radio is on the bench." — no repeats.
  - Perform CPR (wrong): violation recorded on `prioritised_help` ("CPR started before help was called"); the radio stays usable and `send_for_help` is graded whenever they eventually call (likely late → warning).
  - While the card is open the casualty pills hold off; they resume after the pick. Card also closes if the radio gets used first.
- The dormant decision system (`decision_screen.gd`, `help_before_cpr.tres`) stays untouched/dormant.

## 6. Post-shock "check for signs of life" pill
- `scripts/ui/casualty_action_menu.gd`: new CALLOUTS entry `{id: &"signs_of_life", anchor: &"mouth", label: "Check for signs of life"}` with a rule = CPR state is COMPRESSIONS_2 and not yet clicked (the menu already refreshes on `cpr_state_changed`, and no other callout is live at that state, so it shows alone).
- Click: centre message "No signs of life." + `Events.log_action(&"resuscitation", "Checked for signs of life after the shock", &"warning", "Compressions should resume immediately after a shock.")`, then the pill retires. Compressions are never blocked; ignoring the pill logs nothing.
- Build-time check: confirm the pill click doesn't collide with the compression minigame's `interact` handling (pill sits above the chest aim zone; verify in game and adjust hit priority if needed).
